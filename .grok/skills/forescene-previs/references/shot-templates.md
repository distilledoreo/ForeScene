# Shot and camera templates

| Template | Still guidance | Motion pairings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `establishing` | Very wide environment. | Slow drift or crane. |
| `wide` | Environment plus figures. | Entrance, exit, or measured lateral move. |
| `full` | Full body. | Locomotion or meaningful crossing. |
| `medium` | Waist-up. | Restrained push-in. |
| `medium_close_up` | Chest-up. | Subtle reaction drift. |
| `close_up` | Head and shoulders. | Very subtle movement only. |
| `extreme_close_up` | Face detail. | Usually still; avoid pretending to author facial acting. |
| `two_shot` | Requires at least two camera subjects. | Lateral or restrained dolly. |
| `over_the_shoulder` | Requires `foregroundSubject`. | Small push or rack-like positional change. |
| `insert` | Prop or detail. | Controlled reveal. |
| `profile` | Side angle. | Conservative lateral move. |
| `low_angle` | Hero or power. | Slow push or orbit when spatially meaningful. |
| `high_angle` | Looking down. | Small crane or reveal. |
| `overhead` | Top-down. | Controlled orbit or layout reveal. |

Angles: `front`, `three_quarter`, `profile`, `rear`.

Lens classes: `wide`, `normal`, `long`.

Choose the camera template for composition first. Add motion only after the initial compile and only when it communicates timing or spatial change that a still cannot.
